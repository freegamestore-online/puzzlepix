# PuzzlePix — store listing

**Title:** PuzzlePix
**Category:** Puzzle
**Type:** Standalone (no backend for play; the daily time is posted to the platform leaderboard; everything else is saved in the browser)

**Short description:** Jigsaw puzzles of pictures the game draws itself from a number. Solve today's daily puzzle, or cut any picture into 12 to 150 pieces. Free, no tracking.

## Description

PuzzlePix has no picture files to download. Every picture is generated in your browser from a seed, so the same seed always draws the same picture.

- A new daily jigsaw every UTC day, cut into 24 pieces, with a timed leaderboard
- Practice any of 60 pictures in four themes (landscapes, gradients, geometric, mosaic scenes) at 12, 24, 48, 96 or 150 pieces
- Optional turning of pieces in 90° steps, and a tray that is either scattered or sorted edges-first
- Ghost, edges-only and one-piece hint helpers. A daily time with a hint does not go on the leaderboard
- Pieces snap into place when they are close enough, and a finished puzzle reveals its picture
- Your puzzles are saved in your browser, so you can pick up where you left off
- Works offline once loaded, in light and dark themes
- Free forever, MIT-licensed, with no ads, accounts or tracking
